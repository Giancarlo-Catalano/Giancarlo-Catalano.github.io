Information about Ramadan

Ramadan Dates: The month of Ramadan is expected to start around loading... and end around loading....

Practical considerations for students:

  • Fasting: Students abstain from all food and drink (including water) from dawn (Fajr) until sunset (Maghrib).
  • Energy Levels: You may notice students being tired, dehydrated, or struggling with concentration, especially during late afternoon classes.
  • Breaking the Fast (Iftar): If you have classes around sunset, students will need to break their fast immediately. It is helpful to allow them to eat a small snack in class, or grant a short 10-minute break.
  • Friday Prayers (Jumu'ah): Takes place early Friday afternoon. This is a congregational prayer requiring a sermon. Students usually need to travel to a mosque or designated large hall, meaning they will need more time away from the building.
  • Assessments: Some students may require slight adjustments if assessments fall late in the day when their energy is lowest.
